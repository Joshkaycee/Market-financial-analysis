Project Overview
This project analyzes customer demographics, purchasing behavior, spending patterns and marketing campaign performance using Power BI.
The dashboard was designed to provide an interactive view of customer value, purchase activity, campaign response and spending behavior, helping identify patterns that can support data-driven marketing and customer retention decisions.

Business Problem
A company wants to better understand its customers and evaluate the effectiveness of its marketing campaigns. However, customer demographics, purchasing behavior, spending and campaign responses need to be analyzed together to identify valuable customer segments and opportunities for improving marketing performance

Objectives
Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
Identify high-value customer segments.
Compare spending patterns across customer groups.
Evaluate campaign acceptance and response.
Analyze purchasing channels.
Identify relationships between customer spending and deal purchases.
Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

Key Insights:
## Customer Insights

- The 40–49 age group represents one of the largest customer groups in the dataset.
- Customer spending varies considerably across age groups.
- High-value customers represent an important contributor to overall spending.
- Customer value segmentation reveals meaningful differences in spending behavior.

### Marketing Insights

- Campaign acceptance varies considerably across the five campaigns.
- Store purchases represent the largest purchasing channel.
- Customer spending differs between campaign responders and non-responders.
- Campaign response patterns provide opportunities for more targeted marketing strategies.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve customer engagement and marketing performance:

1. **Prioritize high-value customers**
   
   Develop retention strategies and personalized offers for high-value customer segments.

2. **Improve campaign targeting**
   
   Investigate differences in campaign acceptance and refine targeting, messaging, and offers for lower-performing campaigns.

3. **Leverage strong purchasing channels**
   
   Use insights from the strongest purchasing channels to guide future marketing and promotional strategies.

4. **Personalize customer engagement**
   
   Use customer demographics, spending behavior, and campaign response to create more targeted customer segments.

5. **Monitor campaign performance**
   
   Track campaign acceptance and customer response over time to identify which campaigns generate stronger engagement.
